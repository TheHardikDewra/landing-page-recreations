Frequently asked questions

Answers to what practices ask before they switch. Anything else, put it to us on the call.

What is Chairside, and what does it actually do?
It answers your practice phone. It books, moves and cancels appointments in your software, takes messages for named staff, quotes your own fee policy, and transfers to a human the moment a call needs one. It never gives clinical advice.
How does the setup process work?
Nine days on average. A 45 minute call to capture your rules, five days of shadow mode where it listens without answering, one transcript review you sign off, then it goes live on the hours you choose.
Which practice systems do you integrate with?
Two-way with Chartwell, Molar OS, Opaline, ArchDesk, Quadrant, Periosoft, Dentiq, Sextant and Riva PMS. Anything else is one-way into a shared calendar until we scope it.
What does it cost?
$399 a month for one location and up to 1,200 answered calls, then 22¢ a call. No setup fee, no annual contract, and your number is never held hostage.
