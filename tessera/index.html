Where AI actually helps with licensing

Every platform says AI. Here is the specific work ours does.

Requirement matching

Every address is matched against state, county and city rules for your specific activity, so a bakery with a patio gets a different list than the same bakery without one.

Form filling

Applications are drafted from records you already gave us: EIN, officers, square footage, seating, equipment. You review a finished packet instead of a blank PDF.

Expiry monitoring

Every license, permit and registration is tracked to its own renewal window, with the filing started before late fees or a suspension can start.

Answers without a ticket

Ask Tess what a county requires, what a fee will be, or where a certificate is filed. It reads your own records and our agency library, and it cites the rule it used.

Agency rules kept fresh

3,100 agency sites are re-read every week. When Clark County changes a fee schedule or a form number, your renewal packet changes with it.

When it turns into a judgment call, a zoning appeal, a grandfathered use, a clerk who wants a wet signature, that stays with our licensing team, not the model. You get someone who has filed in that county before.
